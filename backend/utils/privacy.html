How we use it

  • To run Nairawise: saving your transactions and calculating your totals.
  • To warn you when your spending is close to, or above, your income.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure, and to fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We rely on your consent and on the need to provide the service you signed up for. We do not use your financial data for advertising or profiling.

Receipt scanning

When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to our server so the text can be read and the amounts picked out. You then choose which figure to use for your transaction.

We do not keep the receipt image after the amounts have been read. Text recognition is handled by [OCR provider, or “our own server”].

Who we share with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run Nairawise, and only as far as they need to.

  • Hosting and database: [provider name]
  • Receipt text recognition: [provider name]
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect people from harm.
  • A future owner of Nairawise, if the business is ever sold. Your rights in this policy would carry over.

Keeping it safe

Data moves between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed, and every request is checked so you can only reach your own transactions.

No system is perfectly secure. Choose a strong password, keep it private, and log out on shared devices.

Deleting your data

You are in control of what stays.

  • One transaction: open it on the Transactions screen and tap the bin icon, or press and hold it.
  • Everything: tap Clear all on the Transactions screen.
  • Your account: email us at [support email] and we will delete your account and its data within [30] days.

Deleted transactions are removed from our live database straight away. Backup copies are overwritten within [number] days.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of your personal data
  • Correct data that is wrong
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Move your data to another service
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

We give the same rights to users elsewhere where their local law applies. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Nairawise is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.
